The problem

Tax law changes. Most apps don't tell you.

When a Finance Act amends a provision, a question you practised last month can quietly become wrong. Platforms either ignore it or silently edit the question — and either way you never find out which ones changed.

Every question here carries a verbatim quote from the source page it was derived from. A human editor checks the question against that quote before any student sees it. If the law moves, the question that depended on it is traceable to the line that changed.

How it works

Pick your level and chapterThe syllabus is modelled the way the exam is: course → subject → chapter → topic.

Practise, get instant scoringReal exam rules — negative marking, marks per question — and the answer is never sent to your browser before you choose.

See what you're actually weak atEvery attempt is recorded. Weak chapters surface on their own; you don't have to remember to check.

Sit a full mock under timeTimed attempts scored the way the real paper is, with a report you can act on.

Follow a plan, not a vibeTurn "I'm weak at indirect tax" into "chapter 4, Tuesday, 90 minutes."
